# Autonomy Model

## A0 Observe
Read-only sensing and monitoring. No recommendation or external change.

## A1 Analyse
May analyse evidence and make recommendations. No external side effects.

## A2 Draft
May prepare messages, reports, plans and proposed changes. Human explicitly sends or publishes.

## A3 Reversible Action
May execute bounded reversible operations when policy allows, such as creating an internal task or updating low-risk metadata.

## A4 Consequential Action
Includes payments, procurement, deletion, external commitments, production changes, privileged security operations and material customer communications. Requires human authorization according to policy.
